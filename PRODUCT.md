# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Home service contractors (HVAC, roofing) who just booked a discovery call with Luxon Agency. Blue-collar business owners, 35-55, skeptical of marketing agencies, time-crunched, used to being burned by vendors who overpromise. They're looking at this page right after booking - high intent, mild anxiety about whether it was worth their time.

## Product Purpose

Pre-call page. Someone has already booked; this is what they land on between booking and the call. It confirms the booking, plays a short video, sets expectations for the twenty to thirty minutes, tells them what to have ready, and prints the account numbers so Luxon is proven before a stranger asks for money. No pricing and no second call to action, because the call is already booked.

The single metric this page moves is SHOW RATE.

## Brand Personality

Elite, direct, proven. The energy is a top contractor who happens to also run ads - no-nonsense, confident, not "agency-y". The design should feel like a Rolex store, not a marketing funnel.

## Anti-references

Generic purple-gradient SaaS landing pages. Contractor websites with stock photos of smiling plumbers. "Marketing agency" aesthetics with floating icons and buzzword carousels. Anything that makes the page feel like a template. ClickFunnels-style countdown timers.

## Design Principles

1. **Authority through restraint** - less movement, more confidence. Premium brands don't beg.
2. **Specificity builds trust** - concrete copy over marketing vague. Only verified figures ever appear, trade named and client never.
3. **Preparation over persuasion** - someone who arrives with their numbers is the whole game. No scarcity, no urgency devices.
4. **The call is the conversion** - everything on this page serves one job: get them to show up.
5. **Gold is used sparingly** - when gold appears, it means something. Not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. All interactive elements keyboard-accessible. Touch targets 44x44px minimum.
