Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Luxon collects when you use this site or apply to work with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Luxon is a marketing agency based in Clovis, California that runs Meta advertising for home service and other local businesses. In this policy, "Luxon", "we" and "us" mean Luxon, and "you" means anyone who visits this site or submits information to us.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out our application, book a call or contact us, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Business information, such as your business name, trade, service area, team size and typical job size
  • Your answers to our application survey
  • Anything else you choose to tell us, such as the time you book for a call

Information collected automatically. When you visit, we and our service providers collect standard technical data: your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, the link or ad that brought you here (including UTM tags and ad click IDs), and the date and time of your visit.

3. How we use your information

  • To review your application and decide whether we are a good fit
  • To contact you about your inquiry by phone, text message and email, including to schedule and confirm calls
  • To provide, manage and improve our services if you become a client
  • To measure how our own advertising performs and to show relevant ads to people who visited this site
  • To keep our site secure, prevent spam and fraud, and meet legal obligations

4. Text messages and calls

By submitting your information, you agree to receive calls and text messages from Luxon about your inquiry, including by automated means. Message frequency varies. Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. We do not sell or share your mobile information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of buying any service. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, and reply HELP for help. You can also reach us at luxon.ag3ncy@gmail.com. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties.

5. Meta Pixel, cookies and similar technologies

This site uses cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel. The Meta Pixel lets Meta Platforms, Inc. record that you visited, viewed a page, submitted an application or booked a call, so we can measure our ads and show ads to people who have visited. Meta may combine this with other information it holds about you under its own Data Policy.

Our site, forms and scheduling run on GoHighLevel (LeadConnector), which also uses cookies to keep forms and bookings working and to record where a visit came from.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can control how Meta uses data for ads in your Facebook ad preferences. Blocking cookies may stop some features, such as the booking calendar, from working.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run our business, such as our CRM and scheduling platform (GoHighLevel), email and SMS delivery providers, and Meta for advertising measurement. They may use it only to provide services to us.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of Luxon or others
  • As part of a merger, sale or transfer of our business, in which case this policy continues to apply

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, usually for the length of our conversation or working relationship and up to three years afterwards, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing luxon.ag3ncy@gmail.com. California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know, delete and correct personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We will respond within the time the law requires.

9. Do not sell or share my personal information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do use the Meta Pixel (section 5) to measure our ads and show ads to people who visited this site. California law can treat that as "sharing" personal information for targeted advertising, so you can opt out of it.

To opt out, email luxon.ag3ncy@gmail.com or text (559) 558-0886 with "Do not sell or share", plus the name and email or phone number you gave us. We will stop using your information for targeted advertising within 15 business days and confirm when it's done. You don't need an account, and we won't ask for more information than we need to find your record.

You can also block the Meta Pixel yourself by turning off third-party cookies in your browser or using your Facebook ad preferences.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

This site is meant for business owners and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be posted on this page.

13. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information: luxon.ag3ncy@gmail.com or text (559) 558-0886. Luxon, Clovis, California.
