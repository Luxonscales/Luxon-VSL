Questions

Straight answers.

How much does it cost?

Most clients invest from $1,200/month for management, plus ad spend paid directly to Meta from your own account, at least $50/day (about $1,500/month). You'll know the exact numbers on the call, before you decide anything.

Do you guarantee results?

Yes. 15 qualified leads in your first 30 days, or we keep working for free until you get them. It holds as long as you spend at least $50 a day, call every lead within 5 minutes, and approve new ads within 48 hours. It covers leads, not booked jobs or revenue.

How fast will I see leads?

Most accounts start getting leads in the first week. The first 30 to 90 days are about finding the ads and audience that bring booked jobs, not just leads.

Whose ad account is it?

Yours. The account, the ads and the data stay with you, whatever happens.

Who makes the ads?

We film them at your real jobs. No stock footage.

What happens after I apply?

If it looks like a fit, you'll pick a call time right away. We'll text you to confirm. On the call, you'll see a live ad account and what your competitors are running.
